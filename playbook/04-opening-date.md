# 04 — Choose the opening date

**Goal:** the person has decided the date from which IsaFlow holds the books, on evidence.

1. The person signs in to Sage One and opens the company. Confirm it is the right company (name, VAT number).
2. Establish: the first transaction date in Sage; Sage's opening balances (Sage → Opening Balances); the latest
   **signed** AFS and its year-end; whether Sage's year-end balances match those AFS.
3. Propose one of:
   - **Full history** — from the first Sage transaction, if Sage's opening balances are zero. No take-on balances.
   - **From the last signed AFS year-end + 1 day** — take on the audited/compiled balances (the AFS are the
     validation target), then rebuild from there. Reconcile Sage's TB to the AFS line by line first; every
     difference must be explained (rounding, audit adjustments) before posting.
4. Write the evidence and the proposal in the brief.

**Stop:** the person decides. Record the decision, who made it and the date in the brief.
