# 02 — Documents

**Goal:** every document the migration needs is in the company folder, or the register says exactly where it is
or that it is missing.

1. Work through `00_Document_Register.md`. Look, in this order:
   - the company folder and any shared drive the person points you to;
   - **sister / group companies' folders** — ID copies and KYC packs are often filed under another company;
   - email and document search tools you have access to (search by registration number, VAT number, name);
   - only then ask the person.
2. Copy (never move) what you find into the right folder. Name files plainly
   (`COR14.3 <name>.pdf`, `ID_<Surname>_<Initials>.pdf`).
3. **Verify, don't assume:** read each document. Director ID numbers must match the CIPC certificate (COR14.3 or
   the latest COR39). Note certification dates — banks and SARS often want copies certified within 3 months.
4. Fill section 1 of the company's `AGENTS.md`: every fact with its source (document, date).
   A VAT number from a supplier invoice is weaker evidence than SARS's VAT103 — say which you have.
5. Documents that are only in a mailbox you cannot open: record mailbox, date, sender, subject and attachment name.

**Stop:** show the person the gaps and exactly what they need to do (download from eFiling, CIPC, bank, forward an
email). Don't block the rest on missing documents unless the step needs them.
